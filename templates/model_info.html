Engineering Mini-Project Viva & Technical Defense

Campus placement decisions are governed by non-linear thresholds (e.g. eligibility cutoffs where having 1 active backlog abruptly lowers eligibility regardless of high CGPA). Random Forest is an ensemble of decorrelated decision trees that naturally captures feature interactions, resists overfitting via bagging (bootstrap aggregation), and does not require linear separability. Deep neural networks would be prone to overfitting on a tabular dataset of this scale without yielding superior interpretability.

The machine learning pipeline encapsulates StandardScaler and RandomForestClassifier inside a scikit-learn Pipeline. Fitting occurs strictly on the training partition (X_train), and the learned transformation parameters (mean and standard deviation) are subsequently applied to the held-out test set and real-time inference requests.

Placement probability is an algorithmic classification likelihood (the fraction of decision trees voting 'Placed'). However, an ethical engineering system must avoid giving students a false sense of certainty or anxiety. Therefore, a separate, deterministic 0–100 Placement Readiness Index provides holistic, interpretable feedback across academics, aptitude, coding, and communication.
